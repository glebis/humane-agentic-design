## Brand direction

- **Mood:** warm, human, considered
- **Imagery:** risograph print illustration — limited flat inks, visible grain and paper texture, slight misregistration between layers, hand-pulled feel; no photorealism, no vector slickness
- **Subjects:** hands and tools working on something unfinished, schematic diagrams drawn as if printed, not rendered, small repeated marks that add up to a system, workbenches, drafting surfaces, proof sheets
- **Avoid:** stock-photo people smiling at laptops, robot faces, humanoid AI mascots, glowing brains, glossy 3D blobs and lens flares, fake UI screenshots as decoration, circuit-board and neural-network cliches
